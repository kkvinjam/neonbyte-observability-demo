# TVs, phones and wearables

## OLED vs QLED TVs
OLED has perfect blacks and the best contrast for dark rooms and movies. QLED gets brighter, better for sunny rooms, and has no burn-in risk. For gaming, prefer 120 Hz and HDMI 2.1.

## TV size
Rule of thumb: viewing distance (inches) divided by 1.6 is a comfortable 4K screen size. 55" suits about 2.2 m / 7 ft.

## Choosing a smartphone
Flagships give the best cameras and longest updates; mid-range phones like the Pixelon 9a deliver most of the camera quality at half the price. Foldables trade weight and price for a tablet-size screen.

## Smartwatch vs fitness band vs ring
Smartwatches add GPS, apps and ECG but need charging every 1-2 days. Fitness bands last ~10 days. Smart rings track sleep and recovery without a screen.
