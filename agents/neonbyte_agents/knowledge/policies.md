# NeonByte store policies

## Returns
Any order can be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund to the original card. Returns are started from Order history or by asking the assistant; stock is restocked and the payment is refunded immediately. Opened items are accepted.

## Warranty
Every product carries the manufacturer warranty shown on the product page: 12 months for items under $500 and 24 months for items $500 and over. The warranty covers manufacturing defects, not accidental damage, liquid damage or loss.

## Shipping
Orders are processed within a minute, ship the same day and are delivered in about five minutes in this demo store. Shipping is free.

## Payment
NeonByte accepts all major cards. Declined payments do not create an order and the stock is released. A card ending in 0000 is always declined (useful for testing).

## Price match
NeonByte does not offer price matching.
