# Audio buying guide

## Noise cancelling for travel and commuting
Over-ear ANC headphones block the most noise and last longest (30 h). Earbuds are more portable; adaptive ANC adjusts to surroundings. NeonByte picks: Sonora Studio 700 (over-ear), Sonora QuietPods Pro (earbuds).

## Workouts and running
Choose earbuds with IPX4 or better water resistance and a secure fit. Avoid over-ear headphones for running.

## Home theater
A soundbar with Dolby Atmos and a wireless subwoofer is the simplest upgrade over TV speakers. Connect over HDMI eARC for lossless audio. NeonByte pick: Beatwave Soundbar 5.1.

## Portable speakers
IP67 means dust-tight and survives submersion — good for pool and beach. NeonByte pick: Beatwave Boom Mini.
