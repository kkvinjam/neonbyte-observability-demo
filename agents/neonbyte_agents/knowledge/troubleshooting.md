# Troubleshooting

## Bluetooth earbuds or headphones will not pair
Put the case or headphones in pairing mode (hold the button 5-7 seconds until the light flashes), remove old pairings from the phone, and keep devices within 1 m. Reset by holding the button for 15 seconds.

## Laptop battery drains quickly
Lower screen brightness, close background apps, update the OS and firmware, and check the battery health report. Persistent drops below 80% health within the warranty period qualify for a warranty claim.

## TV has no picture or HDR does not work
Check the HDMI cable supports the bandwidth (Ultra High Speed for 4K 120 Hz), enable enhanced HDMI / input signal plus for that port, and update the TV firmware.

## Phone will not charge fast
Use a USB-C PD charger of at least 25 W such as the Ampere 65W GaN Charger and a certified cable; clean lint from the port.

## Smart home device offline
Confirm 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, move the hub closer, and re-add the device through the Matter pairing code.
