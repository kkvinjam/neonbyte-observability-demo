# Laptop buying guide

## Students and everyday browsing
A Chromebook or light laptop with 8 GB RAM is enough for web apps, documents and streaming. Battery life above 10 hours matters more than CPU speed. NeonByte pick: Quantix Chromebook Go for tight budgets, Voltix AeroBook 14 if the budget allows.

## Creators: photo and video editing
Prioritise 32 GB RAM, a discrete GPU, a color-accurate display (OLED or mini-LED) and at least 1 TB SSD. NeonByte pick: Voltix ProBook 16.

## Gaming laptops
Look for a high refresh-rate display (144 Hz+), an RTX-class GPU with 8 GB+ VRAM and good cooling. Expect 2+ kg weight and shorter battery life. NeonByte pick: Quantix Strix G15 Gaming.

## Portability and battery
Under 1.3 kg and 15+ hours of battery is "carry everywhere" territory. ARM laptops lead on battery life.
